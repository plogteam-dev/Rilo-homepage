Account Deletion

How to delete your account

You can delete your account in the RILO app by following these steps.

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Select Account
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Review the notice and confirm deletion

Deletion process

When you request deletion, your account is deactivated immediately and you can no longer use the service. The following data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

  • Profile information (nickname, profile photo, etc.)
  • Logs and photos you created
  • Place information
  • Follow relationships
  • Other service usage records

Please note

  • Your account is deactivated as soon as you request deletion, and you can no longer sign in or use the service.
  • All data is permanently deleted within 30 days of deactivation and cannot be recovered afterwards.
  • Some records may be retained for the statutory retention period as required by applicable law.
