You can now read records written in other languages, translated.

You can now translate logs and comments written in other languages on RILO. When a post is written in a different language, a "See translation" button appears. Tap it to read the post in your own language, and tap "See original" to switch back to the original text. If you turn on Settings > App > Auto-translate, posts written in other languages are translated automatically, without tapping each time. We'll keep improving so you can share your records more easily, whatever the language. Warmly, The RILO Team
