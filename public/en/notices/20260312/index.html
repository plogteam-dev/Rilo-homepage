The Map tab is now open.

In the Map tab, you can browse the places others have left and the places you've recorded — all at a glance on the map. Switching between "All places" and "My records", you can see what places have built up in this area right now, and what route your day followed. Please note that logs set to "Buddies only" are shown on the map only to the people you've become buddies with. We hope you'll revisit your daily records around the places in them, and look back on both familiar neighborhoods and travel moments in a slightly more special way. We'll be the RILO that keeps your day — connected through places — in memory a little longer, and naturally links those moments together. Warmly, The RILO Team
