Child Safety Standards

RILO (the "Company") does not tolerate child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form on its service. This document describes the standards and procedures the Company follows to keep children safe.

1. Prohibited conduct

The following content and behavior are strictly prohibited on the service.

  1. Posting, sharing, storing, soliciting or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  2. Any content that sexualizes or sexually depicts a minor, including photos, videos, text and emoji
  3. Approaching or building a relationship with a minor for sexual purposes (grooming)
  4. Sexual extortion (sextortion) of minors or any trafficking-related conduct
  5. Promoting, encouraging or directing others to any of the above

2. Minimum age

RILO is available only to users aged 14 and older. Accounts found to belong to users under 14 are removed.

3. How to report

Users can report a user or a post directly in the app.

  1. Open the more (···) menu on a post or profile and tap "Report"
  2. Choose "Child safety" or "Sexual content" as the reason

Outside the app, reports can be sent to the email below. In an emergency, please contact local law enforcement first.

Child safety contact: plogteam.dev@gmail.com

4. How reports are handled

  1. Child safety reports are reviewed with the highest priority, with initial action within 24 hours of receipt.
  2. Content determined to be CSAM is removed immediately and the account is permanently suspended.
  3. Content and account records are preserved as required by law and reported to the relevant authorities, including law enforcement and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  4. The Company cooperates with relevant organizations to protect affected children when needed.

5. Legal compliance

The Company complies with the Act on the Protection of Children and Youth against Sex Offenses of the Republic of Korea and the child safety laws of every jurisdiction in which the service is offered, and cooperates with lawful requests from law enforcement.

6. Changes

These standards may be updated as laws or service policies change. Updates are published on this page.

Effective date: September 21, 2026
