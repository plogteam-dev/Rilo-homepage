Saving places

When you come across a place you love in a log, save it with a single tap of the bookmark button. As you save, add tags to sort places by your own standards — restaurants to try, spots you've been meaning to visit, and more. Just so you know, saved places are only visible to you. No one gets notified, and nothing is shared.

Browsing saved places

You can see all your saved places at a glance in the Memories tab. Tap a tag to see only the places under it, and tap a place to see the logs it appears in.

From places you stumbled upon to spots you've always wanted to visit — keep them all in RILO, ready for whenever you need them. RILO will keep connecting them to your next day. Warmly, The RILO Team
