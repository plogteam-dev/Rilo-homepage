The Feed tab is here!

Hello from RILO — it's so good to meet you! Our third tab, "Feed", has been released. In Feed, you can now see other people's logs, and from now on the logs you post will also be public to others by default. (All of the logs you've uploaded so far have been set to private.) We're also hard at work on visibility settings such as "Follow / Unfollow" and "Friends only". They'll be added very soon, so please bear with us just a little longer. In Feed, you can discover people who share a lifestyle like yours, and enjoy meeting places from a fresh perspective. We hope your day — connected through the places in it — becomes an inspiration to someone, and that someone else's day brings a little flutter of excitement to you. RILO will be here to connect those moments. Warmly, The RILO Team
